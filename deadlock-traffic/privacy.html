Privacy Policy

Deadlock Traffic · by MIAITEC

Effective 9 October 2026.

The short version

Deadlock Traffic does not collect any personal data. We don't know who you are, we don't track you, and there are no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, best level and City Builder progress), your settings (sound, music, lighting and driving side) and replays of your recent games on your device, so it can remember where you are. This never leaves your device, except as described in the next section.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your game progress is also stored in your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it syncs between your devices. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, and we (the developer) cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in your device's iCloud settings.

Purchases

The optional Full Game purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The game only checks with the App Store whether the Full Game has been bought on your Apple Account.

What we don't do

  • No personal information is collected or stored by us.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we use your email only to reply to you, and don't share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@miaitec.com
